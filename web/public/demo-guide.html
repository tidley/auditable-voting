Plain-English demo guide

Auditable Voting Demo

This demo takes about 5 minutes. By the end, you will have published a questionnaire, shared an invite, submitted a response, and checked the public result in Observer.

5 minute walkthrough

Open organiser page Open voter page Open observer page

By the end you will

  • Publish a questionnaire as Organiser
  • Share the General invite link or QR code
  • Request a ballot from the real Voter flow
  • Submit a response
  • Check the live submission and final result in Observer

Do this first

Run the demo

Use Organiser, Voter, and Observer. For the cleanest demo, open each role in a separate browser profile, private window, or device.

1

Open Organiser and publish a questionnaire

Open Organiser.

Use Login if you already have an organiser identity, or use New identity for a fresh local demo identity.

Fill in Name, Description, and at least one Question prompt.

When the Readiness checklist is complete, click Publish questionnaire, then click Open vote.

Open the invite and submit your response

The General invite should open the questionnaire and request a ballot automatically.

If the page instead shows Pending invites, click Open + request ballot.

Answer the questions. For free-text questions, use Encrypt for organiser if the answer should not be public.

When the button is ready, click Submit response.

If it is not ready, the button explains why, for example Please answer all required questions or Verifying vote request.

After submission, the page shows Private submission identity and a Submission ID.

Check the submitted vote in Observer

  • Accepted submissions have no Invalid marker

6

Publish and verify the questionnaire result

Observer can show live verified submissions before the final result is published.

For the final published result, return to Organiser and click Close + publish results or Publish results.

Open Observer again and click Refresh.

You can see Questionnaire Results, accepted and invalid response counts, per-question result breakdowns, and Submitted Votes.

  • The reported result is correct
  • Counted submissions are genuine
  • Invalid submissions explain why they were rejected

Why it matters

How is this different from a normal online questionnaire?

Traditional systems require voters to trust the operator and database. With auditable voting, the important checks can be repeated independently.

Question Traditional online voting Auditable voting
Was my response recorded? Trust required You can check the public submission
Was my response altered? Trust required You can compare the published record
Was the result calculated correctly? Trust required Anyone can verify it
Can the organiser secretly change results? Difficult to detect Publicly detectable

Privacy

What other people can see

Your identity

Your submitted response is linked to a private submission identity, not your normal voter identity or real-world identity.

The public record

Responses can be audited, results can be verified, and personal information is not required. Free-text answers can be encrypted for the organiser when configured that way.

If two independent users obtain the same Observer result after refreshing from the same public data, they can be confident they are seeing the same questionnaire outcome.
